- Your Team Agreement should clearly describe how your team will work together.

## 1. Team Values
- What values will guide your team?

respect, accountability, communication, and reliability. All team members are expected to have the desire to excel and are expected to respect the efforts and contributions of others in the team.

## 2. Meetings
- How often will you meet?
- When and where will you meet?
- What happens if someone cannot attend?


The team will meet once a week outside of class to discuss our progress and what we’ve been working on. Meeting time and location will be determined once our team gets a 4th person. If a team member is unable to attend the meeting, they will be expected to inform the others about what they are working on via the discord.


## 3. Communication
- What communication tools will you use?
- Which tool will be used for urgent matters?
- How will important information be shared with the entire team?

Our team will have a Discord Chat to allow team members to contact each other. Any urgent matters can also go through the Discord. 
Good communication will prevent the majority of problems that arise in group settings as well as solve any problems that do occur; team members will be expected to keep in touch with their fellow teammates and clearly discuss any concerns they have as well as their current progress.


## 4. Response Expectations
- How quickly should team members respond to messages?
- What should someone do if they cannot respond?
- What happens if a member repeatedly fails to respond?

Team members are expected to check the Discord regularly and respond to messages within 24 hours of receiving them. If a team member is temporarily unable to contribute or knows that they will not be able to respond (i.e. they are going on vacation) they are expected to give the team a warning. If a team member repeatedly refuses to interact with the team and contribute to the project, the rest of the team may ask that the Instructor adjust the mark of the offending teammate to reflect their inactivity.


## 5. Respect & Professional Conduct
- What does respectful behavior mean to your team?
- What behaviors are unacceptable?
- How will you make sure everyone has an opportunity to participate?

Team members should respect the efforts of their fellow teammates. This means that they should not edit or remove the work of another teammate without talking to them first. If a team member has some ideas about how code may be improved, they should discuss it with the author of the code and they can both decide on how to proceed. 
Team members should use professional and kind language when communicating as each member will be giving their best efforts. The use of insulting or vulgar language against a fellow teammate is not acceptable.
At our meetings, we will discuss any ideas that people have for what can be done to improve our application as well as what has already been implemented. From these discussions, we will assign tasks to each team member so that they may work independently on their own section of the project. By separating tasks, we will have an easier time maintaining the modularity of our projects as well as guaranteeing that no 2 members are interfering with each others work.


## 6. Accountability
- What will your team do when a member does not meet the expectations agreed upon by the team?

We will discuss the issue with all team members. We will need to determine if they were assigned too much work from the get go. If this was the case, we will re-delegate tasks to better distribute the workload. 
If a team member consistently does not pull their weight or does not try to contribute to the project, they will be given a verbal warning from the rest of the team. If the behaviour continues, the rest of the team may need to request that the instructor decreases their mark to reflect this.

## 7. Decision-Making
How will your team make decisions?
Clearly explain:
- How decisions will be made
- Whether you will use consensus, voting, or another method
- What happens when team members disagree


Discussions will be made democratically. Any time that a decision must be made, the group will discuss the issue at hand and each member will have the opportunity to have their side heard. After discussing, the team will vote on the issue.
If no consensus is reached, then the process repeats with more discussions and more votes


## 8. Conflict Resolution
How will your team handle disagreements?
Describe the steps you will take to resolve conflicts within the team before involving the instructor or TA.

Any conflicts amongst the team will be brought up at a weekly meeting. Team members will be given chances to state their thoughts and opinions at meetings. The group will attempt to come to a unanimous decision on how to proceed after hearing the viewpoints of all members.
If the team cannot agree on how it should be resolved, we will reach out to the TA.

## 9. Anticipated Challenges
Identify potential challenges your team may face and explain how you will address them.

Team members are expected to clearly communicate any concerns or problems they are facing with their team. Scheduling and overall commitment issues when it comes to meetings could pose a problem during busy times throughout the term (i.e midterm season). Collaboration with team mates may be initially challenging as the team gets used to working with each other. Unfamiliarity with backend development could impact productivity and efficiency. We will discuss possible solutions as problems arise at our weekly meeting.
